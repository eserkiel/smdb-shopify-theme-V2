# GTA 6 rules check

Go through the user's planned post (clip, title, caption, hashtags, thumbnail) and mark each point ✅ OK, ⚠️ risk or ❌ problem, with a concrete fix. This is guidance, not legal advice. Say so in one line.

## Checklist
1. **Footage**: Is it from a Rockstar trailer or is it the user's own gameplay? Trailer footage can trigger Content ID claims. Lower the risk with short clips and real commentary, analysis or a reaction. Own gameplay is safer.
2. **Music**: Licensed songs from the trailers and in-game radio are the most common reason for claims and muting. Recommend the platform's sound library or royalty-free music.
3. **Title, caption and thumbnail truthfulness**: no "Official", "Trailer 3", fake leaks or fake release information. Fan concepts must be labelled as concept or fan-made.
4. **Branding**: no Rockstar logo or official cover art used so that the post looks like an official Rockstar release.
5. **Age rating**: GTA 6 is rated 18+ (USK 18, PEGI 18). Don't target minors. Use the platform's mature or violence settings where required.
6. **Advertising**: paid, gifted or affiliate content needs a clear label ("Werbung" or "Anzeige" in Germany, "#ad"). Use the platform's paid-promotion toggle too.
7. **Hashtags**: none misleading, none unrelated.
8. **Third parties**: streamer or creator footage needs permission. Check for recognisable people who haven't agreed.

## Output
The checklist with a status per point, the 1–3 most important fixes at the top, and a one-line verdict: ready to post, post after fixes, or don't post.
